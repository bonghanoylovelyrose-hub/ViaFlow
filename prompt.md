General Objective

To develop and implement a Web-Based Water Billing and Loan Meter Management System with Decision Support System (DSS) for Via Vida Water District, designed to centralize, automate, and improve the efficiency, accuracy, and reliability of billing, payment monitoring, consumer management, and service operations.


Specific Objectives

The specific objectives of this project are to:

1. To develop a centralized consumer information management module that securely stores, updates, and manages consumer records.

2. To enable staff to create, update, and maintain consumer profiles, including account details, billing information, and transaction history.

3. To automate the computation of water consumption and billing to minimize errors and ensure accurate billing statements.

4. To implement a payment monitoring system that records, tracks, and verifies payment transactions from various channels.

5. To generate digital and accurate payment receipts and billing statements for consumers.

6. To develop a loan meter management module that tracks issuance, usage, and return of loan meters and links them to consumer accounts.

7. To create an incident reporting and service management module that allows consumers to report issues such as leaks, low pressure, and damaged pipes.

8. To develop a disconnection monitoring system that identifies accounts with unpaid balances and supports enforcement actions.

9. To generate real-time reports and summaries on billing, collections, outstanding balances, and operational performance.

10. To implement a Decision Support System (DSS) that provides insights and recommendations for loan approvals and operational decision-making.

11. To provide a consumer-access platform that allows users to view billing statements, payment history, and request services such as connection and reconnection.

12. To improve overall operational efficiency, data accuracy, and service quality by replacing manual and fragmented processes with an integrated digital system.
