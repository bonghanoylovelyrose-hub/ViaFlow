The filter tabs are live — try clicking Verified, Pending, or Rejected to narrow the feed. Here's what changed and why:
Hero card with a teal left accent. A single dominant balance card with a bold ₱12,400 reads faster than three equal-weight cards competing for attention. The left border stripe (no border-radius, per the rules) adds a calm anchor color without screaming.
Feed rows instead of a table. Each payment becomes a scannable row with a colored dot + icon circle encoding status at a glance. You don't need to read the badge text to understand the state — the color alone tells you. This also collapses cleaner on narrow screens since there are no fixed columns to manage.
Inline filter strip. Moving All / Verified / Pending / Rejected into pill tabs above the list lets consumers self-serve without a separate page or query. The active tab uses a slightly heavier border to stay accessible without relying on color alone.
Modal amount field at 20px. The most important input — the amount — is visually largest in the modal, reducing entry errors. The proof-of-payment area becomes an explicit upload zone with affordance text, which is clearer than a bare <input type="file">.
