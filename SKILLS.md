I. Loan Process and Rules

The organization follows specific guidelines for members and non-members regarding loans:

 * Eligibility: Generally, the borrower must be a member. However, non-members may be allowed to loan depending on the admin's approval.
 * Criterion: There are no specific criteria for the loan other than membership/approval.
 * Interest: There is no interest charged on the loan.
 * Approval: Only the admin has the authority to approve loan requests.
 * Payment Process: * Term: Usually a 1-year period( depending if the customer want to pay for only 3 months or etc.)

   * Frequency: Borrowers can pay on a monthly basis.

II. Disconnection and Reconnection
Guidelines for managing overdue accounts and service restoration:

 * Disconnection Policy:
   * Service is disconnected if there is no payment for 3 months.
   * Once the 3-month mark is reached, a notice for disconnection is issued.
   * The user must pay at least 50% of the total balance once they hit the 3-month mark and they still have an accumulating amount ,they will receive notice of disconnection. 

 * Reconnection Process:
   * A reconnection fee of 200+ is required.
   * The existing balance must be fully settled/paid to restore service.

III. Membership Requirements
To become a member of the association (specifically mentioned as Via-Vida), the following are required:

 * Housing Rights: Must have "Rights sa balay" (House rights) from Pag-IBIG (Housing Loan Contract).
 * Residency: The applicant must be a resident and a member of the local association.
 * Registration: Must be officially registered in the association.

IV. Benefits and Administration
 * Pricing (Member vs. Non-Member): Members enjoy a lower rate per cubic meter.
   * Example: Non-members pay 20 per cubic, while members pay 18 per cubic.
 * Application: Those who wish to join must fill out an application form.
 * Renewal: Membership must be renewed every year.

 * Organizational Roles: (partial)
   * Reader (Meter Reader)
   * Maintenance
   * Collection Body
