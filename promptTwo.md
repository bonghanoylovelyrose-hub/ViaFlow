General Objective
To develop and implement a Web-Based Water Billing and Loan Meter Management System with Decision Support  (DS) for Via Vida Water District to improve billing, payment monitoring, consumer management, and service operations.
Specific Objectives
Specifically, the project aims to:
1. Develop a consumer information management module that:
1.1. Record consumer information
1.2. Update consumer details
1.3. View consumer records

2. Develop a consumer profile management module that:
1.1. Create consumer profiles
1.2. Edit account details
1.3. View transaction history

3. Develop a billing module that:
1.1. Input meter readings
1.2. Compute total bill
1.3. Generate billing result

4. Develop a payment monitoring module that:
1.1. Record payment transactions
1.2. Track payment status
1.3. View payment history

5. Develop a receipt generation module that:
1.1. Generate receipt
1.2. Display payment details
1.3. Print receipt
6. Develop a loan meter management module that:
6.1. Record meter issuance
6.2. Track meter usage
6.3. Record meter return

7. Develop an incident reporting module that:
1.1. Receive issue reports
1.2. Record incident details
1.3. Track issue status

8. Develop a disconnection monitoring module that:
1.1. Check unpaid balances
1.2. Identify overdue accounts
1.3. Generate disconnection list

9. Develop a reporting module that:
1.1. Generate billing reports
1.2. Generate collection reports
1.3. Generate balance reports

10. Develop a Decision Support  (DS) that:
1.1. Evaluate loan data
1.2. Analyze application details
1.3. Provide recommendations

11. Develop a consumer access module that:
1.1. View billing statements
1.2. View payment history
1.3. Request services

Scope and Limitations
  	The proposed system aims to automate and centralize water billing, payment verification, loan management, incident reporting, and service operations to improve billing accuracy, payment monitoring, decision-making, data security, reporting efficiency, and communication within Via Vida Water District. The scope of the project is defined in terms of people, data, processes, and software.
 In terms of people, the system will serve authorized internal users and registered consumers of Via Vida Water District. The administrator will oversee overall operations, manage user accounts, verify uploaded proof of payments from external payment platforms, evaluate loan applications with the assistance of the Decision Support System (DSS), monitor incident reports, track loan records, manage disconnection tagging, process connection and reconnection requests, send notifications through Gmail, post announcements, and generate reports. Authorized staff will assist in recording in-person payments, updating consumer information, and handling service-related transactions. The assigned meter reader will encode meter readings with limited access based on role permissions. Registered consumers will be able to access billing statements, payment history, loan status, submit incident reports, receive email notifications, and request connection or reconnection. Access will be limited only to authorized personnel and registered users within the water district.

    In terms of data, the system will manage consumer personal information, account details, meter readings, computed consumption, billing amounts, Payment Reference Numbers (PRNs), uploaded proof of payments from external transactions, payment verification records, and digital receipts. It will also store loan-related data such as applications, approval status, repayment schedules, and meter assignments. Incident reports submitted by consumers, including details of water-related issues, will also be recorded. Additional data will include disconnection records, connection and reconnection requests, and email-based system notifications. System logs will track user activities such as payment verification, loan evaluation, incident handling, meter reading entries, and report generation to ensure transparency and accountability.

    In terms of processes, the system will support customer information management, meter reading entry, automatic billing computation, loan application and evaluation with DSS recommendations, and payment verification based on uploaded proof of payment from external payment methods. The system does not process payments directly but only monitors and verifies them. It will also generate digital receipts after verification. Additionally, the system will handle incident reporting and tracking, automated tagging of accounts for possible disconnection after two to three consecutive months of non-payment, and processing of connection and reconnection requests. Administrators will be able to send notifications via Gmail, post announcements, and generate financial and operational reports. However, loan approval decisions and disconnection actions will remain subject to administrator confirmation.

    In terms of software, the system will be developed as a web-based application accessible through modern web browsers using internet-enabled devices. It will utilize appropriate frontend, backend, and database technologies. Security measures such as user authentication, password encryption, and role-based access control will be implemented. The system will support email notification services through Gmail for communication with consumers. However, the system will not include mobile application development, SMS notification services, direct integration with banking or payment gateways, artificial intelligence-based automation beyond DSS recommendations, or hardware-related installations.
